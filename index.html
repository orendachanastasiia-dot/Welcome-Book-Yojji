[01] Who we areCompanyYojji

A team built

[04] Your first daysOnboardingDay 1 → Month 1

Small steps.
Real momentum.

You don’t need to know everything on day one. Your first week is about getting comfortable, meeting your people, and understanding the context behind your role.

Stage[01]

Day one

  1. Meet your manager and onboarding buddy
  2. Get access to your core tools and accounts
  3. Learn how your team communicates
  4. Set up your workspace and calendar
Stage[02]

First week

  1. Join team ceremonies and introductions
  2. Review your project or role context
  3. Agree on your first priorities
  4. Ask questions early and often
Stage[03]

First month

  1. Build confidence in your day-to-day work
  2. Get regular feedback from your manager
  3. Share what is helping and what could be clearer
  4. Start contributing your own ideas
Friendly note

There are no “silly questions” here. If something is unclear, ask — someone has probably wondered about it before.

[05] How we workRhythmWorking hours

Clear communication.
Healthy rhythm.

We value focus, transparency, and a healthy rhythm. Teams may work differently depending on their projects, but the principles stay the same: keep people informed, respect each other’s time, and solve problems together.

Day begins09:00 — 11:00[A]
Day finishes18:00 — 20:00[B]

Our standard working day begins between 9:00 and 11:00 am and finishes between 6:00 and 8:00 pm, depending on your start time. We trust you to manage your time responsibly and to stay aligned with your team.
